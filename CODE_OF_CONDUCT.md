# Code of Conduct

This project follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, be constructive, assume good faith, and keep the
project a welcoming place for everyone regardless of background or identity.
Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated.

To report a violation, open a private report through GitHub's
[report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) flow or contact the
maintainers directly. Reports are handled confidentially.
